1Nothing new of your own since 31 August

August was your biggest month on Instagram, and then September went quiet. Instagram rewards accounts that keep going after a hit, and the people who found you through those Reels are waiting for the next one.

How to fix it: Keep a simple rhythm, even 2 Reels a week. A "part 2" of your 44k Reel is the easiest place to start.
