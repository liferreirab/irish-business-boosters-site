What's working

2 things worth fixing

1Your Instagram stopped in July 2024

The last post on @swifts_sliding_wardrobes was over two years ago. Anyone who finds you there will wonder if you're still trading.

How to fix it: Start again with one post a week from recent jobs. You don't need to explain the gap, just get going.

2The account looks personal rather than like a business

Your profile name is "Jamie Swift" and the category is "Carpenter", so someone searching for wardrobes in Waterford may not realise it's Swift's Furniture.

How to fix it: Change the name to "Swift's Furniture | Sliding Wardrobes Waterford" and add a line about free home consultations to your bio.
