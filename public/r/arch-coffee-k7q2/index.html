What's working

  • 2,282 people follow you on Instagram, more than most coffee shops in Waterford. That's a loyal crowd.
  • Over 1,200 posts over the years, and Reels that reached 1,400 to 1,600 people last winter. You know how to do this.
  • A 4.7 star rating on Google, and reviewers mention the free coffee when they buy a bag of beans. That's a brilliant offer to shout about.

3 things worth fixing

1Posting has slowed right down

You've posted 4 times in the last 3 months, with an 8-week gap between 28 July and 22 September. Instagram shows your posts to fewer people when an account goes quiet, so each gap makes the next post work harder.

How to fix it: Aim for 2 or 3 posts a week. Batch them: take 15 minutes on a quiet morning to shoot a week's worth of photos and clips, then schedule them.

2Your Reels are reaching fewer people

Your Reels in December and January got 1,385 and 1,630 views. The ones in June got 930 and 415, and your latest (22 September) has 181 so far.

How to fix it: Open with the best 2 seconds (the pour, the steam, a smile), add a line of text on screen, and keep it under 15 seconds. Posting regularly also brings reach back.

3Your bio only lists opening hours

Your bio is just the hours for Peter Street and Ballinakill. Someone discovering you doesn't learn what makes Arch special, and the link sends them to a website that shows a security warning (more on that below).

How to fix it: Add one line about you (for example "Speciality coffee, roasted beans to take home"), mention the free coffee with a bag of beans, and keep the hours in a Story highlight.

3 post ideas for Arch Coffee

Ready to use this month. No fancy kit needed, a phone is plenty.

"Bean of the week"

A 10-second Reel of you opening this week's bag, with the tasting notes on screen. Ends with: "Buy a bag, your coffee's on us."

"First pour of the day"

Film the 8:30 opening at Peter Street: lights on, grinder, first flat white. Calm, satisfying and easy to make.

"Your usual?"

Ask a regular (with permission) what they always order and why. Real faces get far more reach than product shots.

Also worth a look

Not social media, but it affects people who find you there:

  • Your website shows a security warning. The security certificate on archcoffee.ie has expired, so people who tap the link in your bio see "Your connection is not private". Your web host can renew it, usually for free.
  • Your old George's Street listing on Google still shows as "Permanently closed", with 114 reviews, more than your current listing. Google support can mark it as moved to Peter Street.
  • There's no phone number on your Peter Street Google listing, so there's no "Call" button.
