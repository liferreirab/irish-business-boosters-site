What's working

  • A Reel from September 2025 reached 24,900 people. That's huge for a local gym.

2 things worth fixing

1No Reels since your 24.9k hit

Your last Reel was in September 2025, and it was your best ever. Since then it's been occasional photos, about one a month.

How to fix it: One Reel a week: a class in full swing, a coach's tip, a member's progress. Gyms are made for short video.

2Quiet since 28 July, and old pinned posts

There's been nothing new for two months, and the posts pinned at the top are from 2022 and 2024. September and January are when people look for a gym.

How to fix it: Pin your timetable or current offer, and post 2 or 3 times a week through the autumn.

3 post ideas for MOVE Dungarvan

A short before-and-after or a quote from a member (with permission).
