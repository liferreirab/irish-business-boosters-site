What's working

  • Collaboration posts with Dungarvan Brewing Co, Waterford Festival of Food and local bands put you in front of their followers. That's smart.
  • Your Story highlights are well organised (Specials, desserts, Christmas, food festival).
  • 2,330 people follow you even though you post rarely. They're waiting to hear from you.

3 things worth fixing

1About one post a month of your own

Apart from collaborations, you've posted 5 times since May. Your Google reviewers keep mentioning the mussels (9 reviews), the fish pie (8) and the harbour view, but your followers rarely see them.

How to fix it: Post 2 or 3 times a week. You don't need new ideas: your signature dishes and that view are the content.

2Only 2 Reels this year

Both of your Reels this year went up on 2 June. Reels are what Instagram shows to people who don't follow you yet, like visitors planning a day out in Dungarvan.

How to fix it: Film 10-second clips during service: a dish landing on the table, the quay at sunset from your window. Post one a week.

3Your website doesn't link to your Instagram

interlude.ie has no Instagram or Facebook links, and your Google listing can't show them either (see below). People who find you there never discover your social media.

How to fix it: Add your Instagram and Facebook icons to the website header or footer. It takes minutes.

3 post ideas for Interlude

Ready to use this month. No fancy kit needed, a phone is plenty.

"The fish pie everyone talks about"

A slow-motion Reel of the first spoonful, with a real review quote on screen.

"Table with a view"

A 6-second clip of the quay from your best table at golden hour. Caption: "Book this one for Friday."

"This week's special"

Every Wednesday, a Story or post of the chef plating the week's special. Easy to keep up, and it gives people a reason to come back.
