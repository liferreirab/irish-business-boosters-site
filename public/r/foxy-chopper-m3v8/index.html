What's working

  • A 4.8 star rating from 62 Google reviews. Your clients love the work, and that's the best content a salon can have.
  • You've already set up Story highlights (Salon interior, Clients Hair, Salon Life), so the profile has a good base.
  • L'Oréal colour specialist is a real selling point that most salons in town can't claim.

3 things worth fixing

1Your Instagram has gone quiet

You've posted 3 times since December 2024 (12 December 2024, 26 February 2026 and 12 September 2026). For a salon, Instagram is the portfolio: most new clients look at your recent work before they book.

How to fix it: Photograph every finished look (with the client's OK) and post 2 or 3 a week. A ring light by the mirror makes it a 30-second job.

2No Reels for over a year and a half

Your last Reel was in February 2024. Reels are how salons get shown to people nearby who don't follow them yet, so right now new people aren't finding you on Instagram.

How to fix it: Short before-and-after transformations (under 10 seconds) are the easiest Reels to make and among the most watched in hair.

3Your bio has typos and no way to book

Your bio reads "Boutquie" and "Award Wining", doesn't say where you are, and doesn't tell people how to book. You also follow 2,858 accounts while 1,659 follow you, which can make a profile look less established.

How to fix it: Fix the spelling, add "📍 Gladstone Street, Waterford" and "Book online 👇" above your link, and unfollow accounts that aren't relevant any more.

3 post ideas for Foxy Chopper

Ready to use this month. No fancy kit needed, a phone is plenty.

"Before and after"

A 7-second Reel: the before, a quick spin, the reveal. Caption with the technique and the L'Oréal colour you used.

"Ask the colour specialist"

Answer one question clients always ask ("Will balayage damage my hair?") in 20 seconds, filmed at the chair.

"Meet the team"

One post per stylist: their speciality and favourite look. People book people, not salons.
