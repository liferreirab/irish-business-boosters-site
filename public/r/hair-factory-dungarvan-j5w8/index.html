What's working

  • A 4.9 star rating on Google. Your clients are very happy.
  • 1,700 people follow you on Facebook.
  • Hair, nails and make-up under one roof means endless before-and-after content.

3 things worth fixing

1Your Instagram has been mostly quiet since 2021

There have been 3 posts since 2022 (one in December 2023 and two in April this year). For a salon, Instagram is where new clients check your work before booking.

How to fix it: Photograph finished looks and nails (with the client's OK) and post 2 or 3 a week.

2No Reels since 2018

Reels are how salons get shown to people nearby who don't follow them yet. Without them, the account isn't reaching anyone new.

How to fix it: Short transformations are the easiest Reels to make: the before, a spin, the reveal. Under 10 seconds.

3Your Instagram and Google show different addresses

Your Instagram bio says Emmet Street, while Google lists you at 53 O'Connell Street. If you've moved, new clients may go to the wrong door.

How to fix it: Update your Instagram bio to your current address and add your phone number and how to book.

3 post ideas for The Hair Factory

Ready to use this month. No fancy kit needed, a phone is plenty.

"Nails up close"

A slow 6-second close-up of a finished set, turning in the light.

"Before and after"

Colour or cut transformations, with the technique in the caption.

"Wedding morning"

Hair and make-up for a bride or bridesmaids (with permission). These get saved and shared.
