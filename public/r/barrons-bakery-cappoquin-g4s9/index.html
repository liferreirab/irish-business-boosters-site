2 things worth fixing

1Your Instagram stopped in 2020

@barronsbakery has 5 posts and 111 followers, and the last post was in August 2020. Visitors and walkers planning a stop in Cappoquin tend to look on Instagram, and they won't find much.

How to fix it: Restart the account and share what you already post on Facebook. Meta Business Suite lets you post to both at once.

2Three different names

You're "Barrons Coffee House" on Facebook, "Barron's Bakery" on Instagram and "Barron's Bakery & Coffee House" on Google. That makes you harder to search for and to tag.

How to fix it: Pick one name (the Google one covers both sides) and use it everywhere.
