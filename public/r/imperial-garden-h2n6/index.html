What's working

  • 2,900 people follow you on Facebook. That's a ready-made audience in Waterford.
  • 82% of 376 Facebook reviewers recommend you, and you come up first for "chinese restaurant Waterford" on Google Maps.
  • Google reviewers keep mentioning your early bird menu and the portions. Those are ready-made posts.

3 things worth fixing

1Your latest Facebook post says your online service is down

Your most recent post (13 September) says your online service is down, and the posts before it date back to 2020. Anyone checking your page before ordering sees a "service down" notice as your latest news.

How to fix it: Post a quick update when it's back, then share something at least once a week: a dish, the early bird menu, a busy Friday.

2Your Facebook bio still says "Newly opened"

Your page describes you as a "Newly opened Chinese Restaurant". With over 600 Google reviews, that undersells you, and it makes the page look abandoned.

How to fix it: Change it to something like "Cantonese restaurant & takeaway on Cork Road. Early bird menu daily. Order online at imperialgarden.ie".

3No Instagram, and the space is wide open

We couldn't find an Instagram account for Imperial Garden, or an active one for Shiki or Golden Jade. Kyoto has 151 followers. Whoever shows up properly first will own Chinese food in Waterford on Instagram.

How to fix it: Create an account, and post your Facebook content there too. Meta Business Suite lets you post to both at once.

3 post ideas for Imperial Garden

Ready to use this month. No fancy kit needed, a phone is plenty.

"Early bird for two"

A photo or Reel of the early bird menu on the table with the price on screen. Post it at 3pm when people are deciding about dinner.

"Behind the wok"

A 6-second clip of the flames in the kitchen. It's one of the most-watched kinds of food video.

"Friday takeaway night"

A Friday Story with the bags lined up and "Order now" with your link.
